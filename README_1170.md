# OSTATOK 1.17.0 — Discovery Progression

Long-term progression remains physical and world-driven: better weapons, protection,
carry capacity, weather resistance and medical options enable harder expeditions.
There is no XP ladder or gear score.

## Discovery-first rule

The UI no longer tells the player where specific loot is found or when a target-farm
cache will refresh. Item tooltips show rarity and real item properties only. The map
shows discovered locations and their general danger, but does not expose loot profiles,
recommended equipment, target-farm contents or exact refresh timers. Home supply uses
only the preset selected by the player and never upgrades it automatically from the
chosen destination.

Manual map notes remain available for danger, caches, water and free-form notes, so
knowledge gained by exploration can be recorded by the player rather than supplied by
the game.

Internal loot-risk rules, high-risk rewards and refresh mechanics are unchanged and
remain covered by automated tests.
