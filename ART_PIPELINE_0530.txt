OSTATOK 0.53.0 — ANIMATION / VISUAL POLISH

Goals:
- fix the static-looking player locomotion in 0.52.1;
- replace the blocky player silhouette with a more readable 3/4 survival figure;
- remove legacy doubled-leg rendering;
- make infected use all four locomotion frames;
- preserve the 80x60 player frame contract, atlas rows/columns, collisions and gameplay.

Art remains original procedural pixel work produced for this project. User reference images are direction only and are not copied into runtime assets.
