# dev72 — the crowbar and what is locked

Some doors and some caches in the ordinary town are now locked. A locked
place is a question: is it worth the noise and the time?

- **Монтировка** (crowbar) — a fourth melee weapon: a two-handed bar that hits
  a little softer than the pipe but staggers more, and the one tool that opens
  locks quietly (a short pry, little noise). Found in garages, workshops and
  villages, sold by the Artel.
- **Locked door**: with a crowbar — pried open in about a second and a half,
  moderately loud. Without — shouldered in: costs stamina and is very loud
  (the street hears it).
- **Locked cache** (a cabinet, a locker): only a crowbar opens it, and it holds
  more than an ordinary one - someone locked it for a reason.
- Raiders kick doors in as before; a door they kicked stays open. They do not
  rob locked caches.

## What can be locked
Only ordinary buildings of the procedural town, decided **when a building is
first created**: a door or cache that already exists in a save never becomes
locked. Never in the start area (two sectors round the start), in settlements,
at a player's home or base, at sites, in scenes. About one door in six and one
cache in seven.
